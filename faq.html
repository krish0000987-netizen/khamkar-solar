☀️ Solar Basics & Technology

What is rooftop solar and how does it work?

▼

Rooftop solar consists of photovoltaic (PV) solar panels mounted on your terrace. When sunlight strikes the panels, silicon semiconductor cells convert solar radiation into Direct Current (DC) electricity. An inverter then converts this DC into 230V/415V Alternating Current (AC) electricity compatible with your household appliances or factory machinery.

What is the difference between On-Grid and Hybrid solar systems?

▼

On-Grid (Grid-Tied): Connected directly with your state electricity utility grid via a bi-directional net meter. Excess solar power is exported to the grid for tariff credits. It has the highest ROI and lowest maintenance.

Hybrid: Features battery energy storage alongside grid connectivity. It provides backup power during grid blackouts while still supporting net-metering.

What is an inverter and why is it critical?

▼

The solar inverter is the brain of your solar installation. It optimizes generation through Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT), converts DC power to clean AC electricity, synchronizes voltage frequency with the utility grid, and protects against voltage surges, ground faults, and anti-islanding conditions.

📐 Sizing, Space & Generation

How is the recommended solar system size calculated?

▼

We analyze your past 12-month electricity bills to calculate your average monthly unit consumption. In most parts of India, 1 kW of rooftop solar generates approximately 120 to 130 units (kWh) of electricity per month (approx. 4 to 4.3 units per day). If your monthly consumption is 600 units, a 5 kW solar system is recommended.

How much shadow-free rooftop space is required?

▼

Typically, 1 kW of solar panels requires approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area. A 3 kW system needs around 250–300 sq.ft, while a 10 kW system requires about 800–1000 sq.ft. We also fabricate high-elevation structures so you can retain usable roof space underneath.

Does rooftop solar generate power during cloudy or monsoon days?

▼

Yes. Solar panels generate power from ambient light (diffused radiation) even on overcast days, producing around 25% to 40% of their peak capacity. During heavy rain, you automatically draw any required balance power from the utility grid seamlessly.

🏗️ Installation & DISCOM Net-Metering

What does Turnkey Solar EPC include?

▼

Turnkey EPC covers every stage: on-site shadow assessment, 3D structural CAD drafting, Tier-1 Waaree modules and Vsole/Deye inverters supply, heavy-duty galvanized structural fabrication, ACDB/DCDB protection switchgear, chemical earthing pits, electrical wiring, DISCOM liaison, and bi-directional meter commissioning.

How long does a rooftop solar installation take?

▼

Physical rooftop installation for a residential system (3 kW to 10 kW) typically takes 2 to 4 working days. Commercial projects take 1 to 3 weeks depending on size. DISCOM net-meter inspection and bi-directional meter installation generally takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on regional utility schedules.

🔧 Maintenance & Warranty

How much maintenance does a solar rooftop system need?

▼

Solar PV systems have zero moving parts, making them extremely low maintenance. The primary requirement is cleaning panel surfaces with clean water every 10 to 15 days to remove accumulated dust and bird droppings. Khamkar Solar also provides annual electrical health checks.

What is the expected lifespan and warranty of solar panels?

▼

Tier-1 solar panels like Waaree come with a 25-year linear power performance warranty, guaranteeing at least 80–84% generation output after 25 years. Solar inverters typically come with 5 to 10 year manufacturer warranties with extended options.

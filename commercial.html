Commercial & Industrial EPC Workflow

Every C&I project follows a structured engineering timeline minimizing disruption to active factory operations.

01. Site Feasibility & Survey

Purlin spacing measurement, sheet profile audit, transformer capacity verification, and 3D shadow CAD modeling.

02. Sizing & Structural Design

Wind tunnel structural compliance, non-penetrating seam clamping selection, and electrical single-line drafting.

03. Sourcing & Fitment

High-efficiency Waaree TOPCon / Bifacial panels and Deye/Vsole commercial inverters with heavy-duty switchgear.

04. HT / LT Integration

Safe busbar tapping, dedicated motor protection, Class-II surge suppressors, and multi-pit chemical earthing.

05. DISCOM Liaison & CEIG

End-to-end documentation, chief electrical inspectorate clearance, and bi-directional solar meter installation.

06. Telemetry & Handover

Live cloud energy telemetry configuration, operator maintenance training, and performance guarantee warranty.

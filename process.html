7-Step Execution Methodology

Engineered For Reliability

A disciplined, step-by-step engineering workflow designed to eliminate installation risks, guarantee electrical safety, and ensure long-term generation output.

From First Call to Green Energy

The 7-Step Khamkar Solar Journey

Discover how your rooftop transforms into an efficient, grid-synchronized solar power station.

01
Stage 01

Consultation & Energy Audit

We examine past 12 months' electricity bills, connected load, daytime power usage profiles, and discuss whether an on-grid or hybrid system suits your goals.

02
Stage 02

Site & Shadow Assessment

Our site engineers conduct an on-roof survey measuring true south orientation, roof structural strength, water drainage, and identifying shadow-casting obstructions.

03
Stage 03

System Sizing & 3D CAD Design

We generate custom 3D layout drawings, calculate optimal tilt angles (15°–22° for Indian latitudes), and prepare Single Line Diagrams (SLDs) for DISCOM approvals.

04
Stage 04

Tier-1 Component Procurement

Direct sourcing of Waaree Mono PERC/TOPCon solar panels, Vsole/Deye inverters, heavy galvanized steel sections, and certified solar DC wiring.

05
Stage 05

Precision Structural Fitment

Installation of heavy-duty mounting rails with SS304 fasteners, torque-calibrated module clamping, neat conduit routing, and dedicated chemical earthing pit excavation.

06
Stage 06

Quality & Electrical Testing

Rigorous testing protocols: string Open Circuit Voltage (Voc), Short Circuit Current (Isc), insulation resistance, and AC/DC surge protection verification.

07
Stage 07

Commissioning & Net-Metering

DISCOM inspection, bi-directional net-meter synchronization, IoT cloud mobile app configuration, and complete system documentation handover.
